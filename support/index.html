Stemwyn Support

Stemwyn is a private plant and garden journal for iPhone, published by Akeem Gaskins Rivera, an independent developer.

Get help

Email stemwyn@mail.grokbot.com with your question, device model, and iOS version. We usually reply within a few business days.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? On your iPhone only. There is no Stemwyn account and we do not run servers that receive your journal.
  • How do I unlock Stemwyn Pro? In the app, go to More › Stemwyn Pro, or tap Unlock when you hit the free-tier plant limit. Purchases go through Apple. Use Restore Purchases on a new device after signing in with the same Apple ID.
  • How do I back up? Settings › Data › Back up now, or turn on optional iCloud backup (saves to your own iCloud Drive). Videos stay on the device and are not included in backups.
  • How do I erase everything? Settings › Data › Erase all data, or delete the app.
  • Does Stemwyn give growing advice? No. Stemwyn is a private journal for your own records. Follow product labels and local rules.

About care info

Stemwyn is a journal. It keeps the notes, readings and schedules you enter; it doesn't give professional horticultural, safety or legal advice. Calculators do unit math only. Always follow the label of any fertilizer, nutrient, pest-control or other product, and local rules for what you grow. Keep products away from children and pets.
