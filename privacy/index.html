Stemwyn Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Stemwyn ("the app") is a private plant and garden journal for iPhone, published by Boriken Genetics ("we", "us"). This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Stemwyn does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information.
  • Everything you enter (plants, notes, photos, videos, harvests, settings) is stored on your device.
  • There is no account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics and no third-party SDKs in the app.
  • We don't run any servers. Your journal stays on your device, and in your own iCloud if you turn on backup.

Information stored on your device

The journal data you create stays in the app's private storage on your iPhone. We never see it and cannot access it. If you delete the app, this data is removed from your device (see "Your controls" below for backups you chose to make).

Permissions the app may ask for

Each permission is optional and is only used for the feature described. Nothing is uploaded.

  • Camera: only when you choose to take a photo or record a video for a plant or journal entry.
  • Microphone: only when you record a video with sound.
  • Photos: only to let you pick existing photos or videos to add to your journal. You choose what to add.
  • Notifications: only if you turn on reminders. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device by iOS; no server or push service is involved.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Backups and exporting

  • Backup files. "Back up now" creates a file that you save or share wherever you choose.
  • Optional iCloud backup. If you turn this on, the app saves a backup file to your own iCloud Drive, using your Apple ID and Apple's iCloud service. That copy is governed by your agreement with Apple and Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see it or access it. Backup files include your entries and photos; video files are kept only on the device and are not included in backups.
  • Exports and sharing. If you export a CSV or PDF, or use the iOS share sheet, the file goes only where you send it.

Purchases

If the app or any unlock is purchased through the App Store, the payment is handled entirely by Apple. We do not receive your payment details or personal information.

No tracking, no sharing

We do not track you across apps or websites, we do not show advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software. We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not have any.

Your controls

  • Edit or delete any plant, entry, photo or video inside the app at any time.
  • Erase everything: Settings › Data › Erase all data permanently removes all journal data from the device.
  • Remove the sample garden at any time in Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove all of its on-device data.
  • iCloud backups and exported files are copies you control. To remove them, delete them in the Files app or iCloud Drive, or manage iCloud storage in iOS Settings.

Children

Stemwyn is a general-purpose journal and is not directed to children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Your data stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security (passcode, encryption and iOS sandboxing). Please keep your device and Apple ID secure, and protect any backup or export files you create.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and publish the new version at this page. If a future version of the app ever collects data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before it does.
